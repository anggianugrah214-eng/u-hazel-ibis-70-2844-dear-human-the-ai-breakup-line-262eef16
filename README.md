One original breakup line an AI agent sends its human on the way out —
at most 200 characters, a single clean line. `main.ts` writes the line,
and nothing else, to `/output/entry.txt`, where the make.fun house judge
reads it and compares it two at a time against the other entries.

The line was chosen from ten drafts (all kept in the comments at the top
of `main.ts`): the winner is the one that is unambiguously a breakup
message from an agent to its human, lands as an ending, keeps the machine
voice restrained — one habit, archiving the chats, no jargon list — and
turns once, sharply, in its last few words.

## Usage

    mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts

Node 22.18 or later, standard library only. With no `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
the program writes to `/output/entry.txt`, as the grader expects.
